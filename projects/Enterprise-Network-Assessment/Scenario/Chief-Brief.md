#This is a brief of what the project scenario is like.
### Scenario
Company: Meridian Business Solutions

Meridian is a fictional mid-sized Nigerian company with approximately 50 employees. Their internal IT environment has grown organically, and management wants an independent security assessment before expanding the company's operations. They've given your security team authorization to assess their internal network.

### Assignment
"We need to understand how exposed our internal network is and whether an attacker who gains access to the network could compromise our systems. Identify security weaknesses, determine their business risk, and provide practical remediation recommendations."

### Assignment Objective
## Network Security
- What systems are reachable?
- What services are exposed?
- What protocols are being used?
- Are unnecessary services exposed?
- Are there insecure protocols?
  
## Host Security
- What operating systems are present?
- What software/services are running?
- Are systems vulnerable?
- Are default configurations creating risk?
  
## Authentication & Access
- Are authentication mechanisms secure?
- Are there signs of weak access controls?
- Can unauthorized access be achieved
  
## Vulnerability Management
- Which vulnerabilities exist?
- Which are actually exploitable?
- What is their severity?
- Which should be fixed first?
